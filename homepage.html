Our Story

Coffee crafted
with intention

Sit, Sip & Bite was born from a simple belief — that a great cup of coffee deserves a great space. We opened in 2024 right in the heart of Gaborone, bringing together premium single-origin beans, hand-laminated pastries, and generous sandwiches made fresh every day.

Every detail matters here: the temperature of the milk, the crunch of the croissant, the warmth of the welcome. We're not just a café — we're your corner.
