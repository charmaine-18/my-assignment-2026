Our Story

Born from a single perfect cup

It started simply: a founder who couldn't find a truly great cup of coffee in Gaborone and decided to do something about it. What began as a personal obsession turned into months of sourcing trips across Africa, barista training, and countless test batches before the first cup was served.

In 2024, Sit, Sip & Bite opened its doors at Main Mall. We named it exactly what we wanted people to do: sit down, slow down, and savour something made with genuine care. In a world that rushes, we wanted to be a pause.

"We didn't open a café. We opened a place where Gaborone could breathe, connect, and taste something real."

Today, we serve hundreds of cups a day — every one pulled with the same precision as the first. Our pastries are laminated by hand before the city wakes up. Our sandwiches use fresh, locally sourced ingredients assembled to order. Nothing here is done halfway.

Our Journey

How we got here

Early 2023

The Idea Takes Shape

Frustrated by the lack of truly excellent coffee in Gaborone, our founder begins researching coffee origins, brewing techniques, and the art of pastry-making — often travelling to learn from masters.

Late 2023

Sourcing the Perfect Bean

Months of tasting and trips across East and Southern Africa culminate in a partnership with ethical small-batch Arabica farmers. The signature blend is born — bright, complex, and distinctly African.

March 2024

We Open Our Doors

Sit, Sip & Bite opens at Main Mall, Gaborone. The first latte is served at 7:02 AM on opening day. The queue forms before we unlock the door, and it hasn't stopped since.

2024 – Present

Growing the Community

We expand our menu, host weekly community events, partner with local artists for rotating displays, and build a loyal base of regulars who call our corner their own. This is only the beginning.

How We Work

From bean to cup

Every shot is dialled in fresh each morning. Grind size, dose, extraction time — all calibrated to the day's humidity and bean batch. No guessing, ever.
